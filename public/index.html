Privacy Notice

They're a 10, But... is a free browser game. We collect only the information needed to run the game and display community statistics.

What we collect

  • Your daily game choices and rankings.
  • An anonymous browser identifier to help prevent duplicate submissions.
  • The date and time you complete a game.

How we use it

Your game results are combined with other players' results to calculate community averages, rankings, and daily participation statistics.

We don't require an account, name, or email address to play, and we don't sell your game data.

Storage and privacy

Game statistics are stored using Netlify's hosting and database services. Your individual choices aren't publicly displayed with your browser identifier.
